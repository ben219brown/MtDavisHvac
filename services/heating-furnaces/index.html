Common questions

What should I do if my furnace stops heating?

Call for service and describe the system and symptoms. If you suspect a gas leak or carbon monoxide, leave the building and contact the appropriate emergency service immediately.

How often should heating equipment be serviced?

A preseason check is a sensible general schedule; the manufacturer's recommendations and equipment condition may call for a different interval.

Is emergency service available?

Emergency HVAC service is advertised, but arrival times depend on availability. Call directly to explain the urgency and confirm scheduling.
