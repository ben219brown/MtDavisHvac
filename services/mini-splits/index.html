Mini-Splits & Ductless in Somerset County

Target comfort where you need it

Ductless mini-splits can be an option for rooms that are hard to heat or cool with existing ductwork, additions, or buildings where new ducts would be disruptive. The right approach depends on the space, insulation, equipment and comfort goals.

Discuss cooling and heating together

Many modern ductless systems are heat pumps that offer both cooling and heating. Performance in cold Pennsylvania weather depends on model selection and proper system sizing. Mt Davis HVAC can discuss equipment options and whether a ductless system is suitable for your project.

Common questions

Can a mini-split heat as well as cool?

Many mini-split heat-pump systems do both. Their heating capacity and suitability for a particular home depend on equipment and design.

Do mini-splits always require ductwork?

No. Ductless mini-splits are designed to deliver conditioned air without conventional distribution ducts, although electrical and refrigerant-line installation is still required.

Can I get a quote for more than one room?

Yes. Describe the number and type of spaces you're considering when you call so the right configuration can be discussed.
