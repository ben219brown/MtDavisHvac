Get to the source of cooling problems

Warm air, weak airflow, unusual sounds or an air conditioner that won't start are reasons to schedule a professional assessment. Cooling problems can have different causes, so an equipment inspection should come before a recommendation to repair or replace the system.

Installation and replacement decisions

Replacing an older cooling system is a chance to review the home's existing equipment, comfort goals, installation requirements and potential ductless alternatives. Mt Davis HVAC offers consultations and free project quotes so you can discuss options appropriate to your property before authorizing work.

Plan ahead for the cooling season

Seasonal maintenance is an opportunity to identify developing problems before the hottest weather. If your system is due for service, or you're unsure whether a new installation makes sense, call to discuss the equipment you have and what you'd like to improve.

Common questions

Do I need to replace my air conditioner if it stops cooling?

Not necessarily. A loss of cooling can have several causes. A professional assessment helps distinguish a repairable fault from a system where replacement deserves consideration.

Can a ductless mini-split help cool an addition?

Ductless systems can be an option for additions and spaces that are difficult to serve with existing ductwork. System sizing and installation requirements depend on the building.

Can I request a free quote?

Yes. Call Mt Davis HVAC to discuss the system, property and proposed project before an estimate is prepared.
