Maintenance & Repair in Somerset County

Plan a check before peak season

A general rule is to service cooling systems before summer and heating equipment before winter, subject to manufacturer recommendations. Scheduled inspections can reveal developing issues before severe weather places heavier demands on the system.

Repair when something changes

A system that makes new sounds, runs differently or struggles to maintain temperatures may need attention. Mt Davis HVAC provides repair and maintenance for heating and cooling equipment, including advertised seasonal oil-furnace service.

Keep useful information handy

Knowing your equipment's brand, age, previous work and current symptoms makes a service conversation more productive. Call to discuss maintenance timing, describe the equipment and ask what should be addressed during the visit.

Common questions

When should my HVAC system be serviced?

Scheduling a check ahead of the heating or cooling season is a useful starting point. Always consider the manufacturer's maintenance schedule for the specific equipment.

Does a maintenance visit guarantee there won't be a breakdown?

No. Maintenance can help identify developing problems, but no service can prevent every equipment failure.
