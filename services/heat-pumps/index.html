Heat Pumps in Somerset County

One system for changing seasons

Heat pumps move heat rather than producing it solely through combustion. Many systems provide both cooling in summer and heating in colder weather. Ducted and ductless configurations have different installation requirements.

Choose equipment for your property

Equipment selection should account for the building's heating and cooling needs, insulation, layout, existing system and local weather. A consultation is the right time to discuss how heat-pump options compare with other heating and cooling arrangements.

Common questions

Will a heat pump work in a Pennsylvania winter?

Suitability depends on the specific equipment and a home's heating requirements. Cold-climate models and any needed backup heating should be considered during system planning.

Are heat pumps the same as mini-splits?

Some mini-splits use heat-pump technology. Heat pumps can also be built into ducted systems, so the two terms aren't interchangeable.
