Privacy Notice

How contact information submitted through this website is handled.

Effective date: September 24, 2026.

Information you choose to send

If you use the quote request form or website assistant, we ask for your name, phone number, requested service and project details. The assistant also asks about urgency, property type, service location and preferred contact method. Your email address is optional. Do not submit payment card details, Social Security numbers or other highly sensitive information.

Website assistant and optional AI

The website assistant collects service details, contact preferences and an optional email address. Before anything is sent, you can review the information and consent to submitting it. The assistant uses FormSubmit to email the completed request and chat questions and answers to Mt Davis HVAC.

If you opt in to an AI-generated follow-up question, the assistant may send your equipment category and your description of the issue to an AI service provided by OpenAI, through a secure Cloudflare Worker. The assistant does not intentionally include the separate name, email, phone number or service-location fields in that AI request. However, if you put personal details into the free-text issue description, they may be transmitted as part of that description. Do not include sensitive information. If the AI service is unavailable, the assistant uses a standard question instead.

AI suggestions are automated, not a diagnosis or an emergency response. No completed lead request is sent to the company until you approve the final submission; if you opted in to AI, the equipment description may already have been sent to the AI provider to generate a question. The conversation is not saved by the website between visits, although the third-party services involved may process and retain transmitted information according to their own policies.

How your request is processed

This website uses an external form processing service, FormSubmit, to forward online quote requests and completed assistant submissions to the Mt Davis HVAC inbox. By submitting the form, you send its contents to FormSubmit for processing and delivery to that inbox. FormSubmit states in its documentation that it retains submitted form data for 30 days; Mt Davis HVAC may retain the forwarded messages in its email account as needed to respond to your request and manage customer communications.

Review FormSubmit's service information before submitting if you have questions about this provider. Spam-prevention checks may involve an additional verification step.

How we use contact information

The information you provide is used to respond to service inquiries, discuss quotes, arrange requested work and communicate about your project. Do not use the form for emergencies; call instead.

Website hosting and third parties

The website is hosted through Cloudflare and may rely on standard security and operational logging. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. As with most websites, those providers may process technical information such as request IP addresses when your browser loads their services.

Contact and correction requests

To ask questions about information you submitted or request a correction or deletion, call (814) 926-6646 or use our website contact form.

This notice describes the website's form and optional AI integration. It should be reviewed with the owner whenever the form, analytics or service providers change.
