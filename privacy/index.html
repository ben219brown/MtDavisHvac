Privacy Notice

How contact information submitted through this website is handled.

Effective date: September 24, 2026.

Information you choose to send

If you use the quote request form, we ask for your name, phone number, requested service and project details. Your email address is optional. Do not submit payment card details, Social Security numbers or other highly sensitive information.

How your request is processed

This website uses an external form processing service, FormSubmit, to forward online quote requests to Mt Davis HVAC at markoplixo@gmail.com. By submitting the form, you send its contents to FormSubmit for processing and delivery to that inbox. FormSubmit states in its documentation that it retains submitted form data for 30 days; Mt Davis HVAC may retain the forwarded messages in its email account as needed to respond to your request and manage customer communications.

Review FormSubmit's service information before submitting if you have questions about this provider. Spam-prevention checks may involve an additional verification step.

How we use contact information

The information you provide is used to respond to service inquiries, discuss quotes, arrange requested work and communicate about your project. Do not use the form for emergencies; call instead.

Website hosting and third parties

The website is hosted through Cloudflare and may rely on standard security and operational logging. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. As with most websites, those providers may process technical information such as request IP addresses when your browser loads their services.

Contact and correction requests

To ask questions about information you submitted or request a correction or deletion, contact markoplixo@gmail.com or call (814) 926-6646.

This notice describes the website's current form integration. It should be reviewed with the owner whenever the form, analytics or service providers change.
